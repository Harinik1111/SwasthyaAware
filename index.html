Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to key public health concepts.

What is a balanced diet?

A balanced diet contains optimal amounts of carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, dietary fiber, vitamins, minerals, and clean water to fuel metabolism without causing cellular stress.

Why is handwashing so critical?

Physical hand scrubbing with soap for at least 20 seconds breaks down the lipid envelopes of viruses and bacteria, preventing microbial transfer to your eyes, nose, or food.

How much water should I drink daily?

Most adults need 2.5 to 3 litres of clean water a day, more in hot climates or with physical labour. Urine that stays pale yellow is a good sign of adequate hydration.

How many hours of sleep do I really need?

Adults function best on 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted sleep. Consistent sleep and wake times matter as much as total hours for hormonal and immune regulation.

How often should vaccines be updated?

Childhood vaccines follow a fixed schedule set by national immunization programs; adults typically need boosters (like tetanus) every 10 years and annual doses for certain seasonal vaccines as advised by a doctor.

What is the difference between hunger and thirst signals?

Thirst is often mistaken for hunger. If you feel hungry shortly after eating, or the urge fades after drinking water, your body was likely signalling dehydration, not food need.

How much daily physical activity is recommended?

At least 150 minutes of moderate activity (brisk walking, cycling) per week, spread across most days, supports cardiovascular, metabolic, and mental health.

Are frozen or canned vegetables less healthy than fresh?

Not significantly — frozen vegetables are usually flash-frozen at peak ripeness and retain most nutrients. Canned versions are fine too, provided you rinse away excess added salt or sugar.

When should I see a doctor instead of self-treating?

Seek medical care for fever lasting more than 2-3 days, breathing difficulty, persistent chest or abdominal pain, symptoms that worsen despite rest, or any condition involving young children, pregnant women, or the elderly.

Is table salt or rock salt healthier?

Iodized table salt is generally preferred for public health because iodine deficiency causes thyroid disorders; the mineral content difference between salt types is nutritionally minor.

How can I tell if my drinking water is safe?

Boiling water for at least one minute or using a certified filter removes most pathogens. Cloudy, discoloured, or foul-smelling water should never be consumed even after boiling — switch sources.

Can mental health affect physical health?

Yes — chronic stress and poor mental health raise cortisol levels, weaken immunity, disrupt sleep and digestion, and are linked to higher risk of heart disease. Mental wellbeing is a core pillar of prevention.

Balanced Nutrition Architecture

1) Basic Nutrients & 3-Stage Priorities

Carbohydrates (Energy Source)

Description:

Primary fuel that provides physical energy for cellular operation and daily physical tasks.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Whole Brown Rice, Ragi, Oats, Whole Wheat

Stage 2 (Moderate) White Rice, Refined Semolina, Couscous

Stage 3 (Baseline) Tapioca Pearl, Sugars, White Bread Flour

Proteins (Bodybuilding Blocks)

Description:

Crucial block structure for muscle generation, cell repair, and structural hormone synthesis.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Sprouted Green Gram, Lentils/Dals, Organic Eggs, Fresh Fish

Stage 2 (Moderate) Paneer, Low-Fat Milk, Lean Chicken Breast

Stage 3 (Baseline) Groundnuts, Whey Isolates, Plant Flour Concentrates

Fats (Concentrated Energy & Hormone Support)

Description:

Concentrated energy source vital for cell membrane integrity, structural brain support, and absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Cold-pressed Coconut/Mustard Oil, Pure Ghee, Almonds, Walnuts, Avocados

Stage 2 (Moderate) Refined Vegetable Seed Oils, Dairy Butter, Commercial Cheese

Stage 3 (Baseline) Hydrogenated Vanaspati Ghee, Margarine, Deep-fried Bakery Trans-fats

Vitamins (Defensive Immunity Shield)

Description:

Organic micronutrients that orchestrate biological repair, metabolic rate cycles, and ocular health preservation.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Local Amla, Papaya, Drumstick Leaves, Citrus Fruits

Stage 2 (Moderate) Carrots, Pumpkins, Cultured Yogurt

Stage 3 (Baseline) Synthetic Ascorbic Fortifiers, Encapsulated Complexes

Minerals (Structural & Fluid Regulators)

Description:

Inorganic essential element frameworks ensuring dense bone mineralization, nerve signal transmissions, and cellular fluid balance tracking.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Moringa/Drumstick Greens, Whole Ragi, Sesame Seeds, Seafood, Unrefined Sea Salt

Stage 2 (Moderate) Pasteurized Standard Dairy, Fortified Iodized Table Salt, Tofu

Stage 3 (Baseline) Highly Refined Chemical Salts, Commercial Inorganic Mineral Supplements

Fiber (Digestive Catalyst & Gut Balancer)

Description:

Indigestible plant macro-structures structural framework that optimizes bowel kinetics, keeps glycemic indices stable, and feeds probiotic gut microbiomes.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Whole Oats, Flaxseeds, Raw Leafy Green Salads, Unpeeled Whole Pulses, Apples with Skin

Stage 2 (Moderate) Pearled Barley, Peeled Citrus Fruits, Semi-whole Grain Flatbreads

Stage 3 (Baseline) Ultra-refined White Flour, Powdered Synthetic Fiber Dissolvables

Hygiene & Sanitation Frameworks

1) Personal Hygiene Ecosystem

Bathing & Skin Defense

Description:

Daily bathing with clean water removes sweat, sebum buildup, and accumulated superficial micro-organisms, preventing common fungal dermatological conditions.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Clean Running Water & Unscented Mild Soap

Stage 2 (Moderate) Commercial Scented Body Wash / Shower Gel

Stage 3 (Baseline) Sponge Bathing with Warm Water Rinse

Critical Hand Sanitation Protocols

Description:

Scrub palms together with soap, interlace fingers to scrub the webbing, clean behind hands, under nails, and around wrists for a full 20 seconds before rinsing to break pathogen transmission barriers.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) 20-Second Soap & Running Water Scrub

Stage 2 (Moderate) 60%+ Alcohol-Based Hand Sanitizer Rub

Stage 3 (Baseline) Plain Water Rinse & Wet Wipe Clean

Oral & Dental Hygiene

Description:

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste for two minutes, floss once a day, and replace your toothbrush every 3 months to prevent gum disease and cavities that can trigger wider infections.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Fluoride Toothpaste + Soft Bristle Brush + Daily Flossing

Stage 2 (Moderate) Standard Toothbrush & Salt Water Rinsing

Stage 3 (Baseline) Herbal Toothpowder / Neem Twig Cleansing

Nail, Hair & Foot Hygiene

Description:

Trim nails short and clean underneath weekly, wash and dry hair regularly to prevent scalp infections, and keep feet dry with clean socks to avoid fungal infections like athlete's foot.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Weekly Trimming + Nail Brush Scrubbing + Daily Foot Drying

Stage 2 (Moderate) Regular Scissors Trimming & Shampooing

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Water Rinsing & Manual Nail File Maintenance

Menstrual Hygiene Management

Description:

Change sanitary products every 4-6 hours, wash hands before and after changing, use clean water for personal cleaning, and dispose of used products wrapped and in designated bins — not flushed.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Sterile Menstrual Cups / Medical Grade Organic Pads

Stage 2 (Moderate) Standard Disposable Commercial Sanitary Pads

Stage 3 (Baseline) Boiled, Sun-Dried Reusable Cotton Cloth Pads

Clothing & Bedding Hygiene

Description:

Wash and sun-dry clothes and bedsheets regularly, avoid sharing towels, and change inner garments daily to reduce skin infections and dust-mite exposure.

Food Source Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Hot Water Detergent Wash + Direct Sun Disinfection

Stage 2 (Moderate) Cold Water Machine Wash + Indoor Drying

Stage 3 (Baseline) Hand Soap Soak & Line Drying

2) Environmental Health & Vector Control

Surrounds & Bathrooms

Description:

Scrub bathroom surfaces and home peripheries regularly with disinfectants to eliminate pathogen reservoirs and fungal spores.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Daily Disinfectant Scrub + Dry Venting

Stage 2 (Moderate) Weekly Soap & Bleach Wash

Stage 3 (Baseline) Water Flushing & Sweep Routine

Drainage Inflows

Description:

Maintain free fluid flow lines to clear greywater pools and prevent standing water accumulation around homes.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Closed Underground Pipes & Mesh Traps

Stage 2 (Moderate) Covered Concrete Open Channels

Stage 3 (Baseline) Manual Unclogging of Open Drains

Mosquito & Vector Elimination

Description:

Cover standing water containers and introduce eco-friendly larvicides to break mosquito and pest breeding cycles.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Aero-Screening Nets + Biological Larvicides

Stage 2 (Moderate) Tightly Sealed Containers & Kerosene Layering

Stage 3 (Baseline) Manual Emptying of Stagnant Pots

Rodent & Pest Control

Description:

Store food in sealed containers, seal wall/floor gaps, and clear clutter piles that provide rodent nesting sites.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Air-Tight Metal Containers + Structural Hole Sealing

Stage 2 (Moderate) Mechanical Traps & Ultrasonic Deterrents

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Food Covering & Routine Sweeping

Safe Water Storage

Description:

Store drinking water in covered containers, clean tanks every few months, and treat questionable sources with boiling or filtration.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Boiled Water + Sealed Stainless/RO Storage

Stage 2 (Moderate) Chlorinated / Filtered Water in Covered Clay Pots

Stage 3 (Baseline) Cloth-Filtered Water in Lidded Buckets

Indoor Air & Ventilation

Description:

Ensure cross-ventilation, minimize indoor smoke from cooking fires, and open windows daily to reduce respiratory illness risk.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Exhaust Fans + Open Cross-Ventilation Windows

Stage 2 (Moderate) Ceiling Fan Air Circulation & Regular Window Openings

Stage 3 (Baseline) Passive Door Vents & Smoke Chimneys

3) Waste Disposal

Segregation at Source

Description:

Separate waste into wet (kitchen/organic), dry (recyclable), and hazardous streams at the point of generation.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) 3-Bin System (Wet, Dry, Sanitary/Hazardous)

Stage 2 (Moderate) 2-Bin System (Wet vs Dry Separation)

Stage 3 (Baseline) Single Bin with Bagged Organic Waste

Composting Organic Waste

Description:

Kitchen and garden waste can be composted in a covered pit or bin, reducing landfill load and producing fertilizer.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Aerated Pit Composting with Microbial Inoculants

Stage 2 (Moderate) Earthen Pot / Barrel Home Composting

Stage 3 (Baseline) Simple Garden Pit Burial

Biomedical & Sharps Waste

Description:

Used needles, bandages, and expired medicines must never go into regular household bins — return them to health centers.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Puncture-Proof Sharps Box + Clinic Handover

Stage 2 (Moderate) Hard Plastic Container Disposal with Label

Stage 3 (Baseline) Thickly Wrapped Paper Disposal in Red Bin

Plastic & Non-Biodegradable Reduction

Description:

Reduce single-use plastic, reuse containers where safe, and hand over recyclables to authorized collectors rather than burning.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Zero Single-Use Plastic + Cloth Bags + Scrappage Sales

Stage 2 (Moderate) Reusing Rigid Plastics & Municipal Dry Collection

Stage 3 (Baseline) Bundling Dry Waste for Local Recyclers

Preventive Measures & Clinical Safeguards

1. Personal Preventive Practices

Description:

Consistently practice foundational habits: wash hands frequently, consume a balanced diet rich in micronutrients, drink safe water, get 7-9 hours of restful sleep, and engage in daily physical exercise to maintain metabolic efficiency.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Daily 30m Exercise + 8h Sleep + Balanced Diet

Stage 2 (Moderate) Light Daily Walks + Regular Hydration

Stage 3 (Baseline) Intermittent Rest Periods & Adequate Water Intake

2. Vaccination & Immunization Tracking

Description:

Vaccines train the immune system to recognize and fight pathogens safely. Following childhood and adult immunization schedules, along with recommended booster doses, provides durable protection against major infectious conditions.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Complete Childhood Schedule + Annual Flu & Adult Boosters

Stage 2 (Moderate) Core Childhood Immunizations Only

Stage 3 (Baseline) Emergency Post-Exposure Vaccines (Tetanus, Rabies)

3. Communicable vs. Non-Communicable Disease Defense

Description:

Communicable: Prevent droplet-spread illnesses (like Flu, COVID-19, and TB) through cross-ventilation, masking, and good hand hygiene. Recognize early symptoms like prolonged coughing or fever and seek medical care early.


Non-Communicable: Address chronic health concerns (Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, and Heart Disease) through physical activity, balanced diets, and regular biometric tracking.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Active Masking + Routine Biometric Screening

Stage 2 (Moderate) Symptom-based Isolation & Dietary Restrictions

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Medical Consultation upon Symptom Onset

4. Regular Health Screening & Checkups

Description:

Annual blood pressure, blood sugar, and BMI checks catch problems before symptoms appear. Adults over 40 should add cholesterol screening and age/gender-appropriate cancer screenings on a schedule set by their doctor.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Annual Full-Body Panel + Monthly BP/Sugar Checks

Stage 2 (Moderate) Bi-Annual Doctor Consultation & Basic Blood Work

Stage 3 (Baseline) Targeted Screening When Symptoms Arise

5. Mental Health & Stress Safeguards

Description:

Build daily stress-management habits (breathing exercises, adequate sleep, social connection) and treat persistent low mood, anxiety, or sleep disruption as a medical matter worth discussing with a professional.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Daily Mindfulness + Professional Counseling Access

Stage 2 (Moderate) Regular Social Connection & Recreational Breaks

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Rest & Journaling Routines

6. Occupational & Environmental Safety

Description:

Use protective gear appropriate to your work (masks, gloves, footwear), avoid prolonged exposure to smoke, chemicals, or extreme heat without breaks, and report unsafe conditions early to prevent long-term illness.

Action Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Certified PPE Gear + Ergonomic Workstation Adjustments

Stage 2 (Moderate) Standard Dust Masks & Regular Break Schedules

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Posture Awareness & Hydration

Prevention at a Glance

Hygiene

Description:

Hands, water, food, and surroundings kept clean daily to establish biological barriers against bacterial and viral transfer.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Soap Handwashing + Purified Water + Clean Home

Stage 2 (Moderate) Sanitizer Usage + Covered Water Storage

Stage 3 (Baseline) Basic Water Rinsing & Sweeping

Immunization

Description:

Vaccines on schedule for children and timely boosters for adults to train cellular antibody responses against major diseases.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Up-to-date Schedule + Annual Seasonal Boosters

Stage 2 (Moderate) Core Mandatory Vaccines Completed

Stage 3 (Baseline) Post-Injury Tetanus Prevention

Screening

Description:

Routine health checkups to catch silent chronic conditions early before irreversible organ damage occurs.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Annual Comprehensive Diagnostics & Lab Panels

Stage 2 (Moderate) Bi-Annual Blood Pressure & Glucose Checks

Stage 3 (Baseline) Symptom-Driven Physician Visits

Mental Wellbeing

Description:

Stress management and emotional support integrated as part of everyday health to maintain nervous system balance.

Implementation Priority Stages:

Stage 1 (Best) Daily Meditation + Counseling + Quality Sleep

Stage 2 (Moderate) Regular Exercise & Hobbies Engagement

Stage 3 (Baseline) Work-Rest Boundaries & Basic Downtime
